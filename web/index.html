latex2quiz converter

Shows the problems of a LaTeX exam (using specific macros) and exports them to Canvas (QTI) or WISEflow, entirely in your browser. Prototype.

Files

Select the problem set's .tex file plus any files it includes, such as figures (ad-problems-2024-april-figs.pdf) or skriftlige.tex.

The problems are written in the LaTeX format described in the documentation, with Danish or English macro names; the language of the instructions follows the macro names.
